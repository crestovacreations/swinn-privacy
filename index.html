Swinn: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026
Privacy, by design. Swinn was built without a server, so there's no account, no login, and no cloud copy of your financial life sitting on someone else's computer. There's simply nowhere for it to go. Everything you track stays on your device, under your control, always. No analytics, no ad trackers, no data to sell, because there's nothing about your finances for us to have.

1. Your Data Stays With You

Swinn doesn't collect any personal or financial data. There's no sign-in and no server behind the app. Every expense, budget, recurring payment, category, and note you add is saved directly on your device and never sent anywhere else. We built it this way on purpose: your financial life is personal, and it should stay that way.

2. Where Your Data Lives

Your data lives in Swinn's local storage on your device. It's yours, not ours. Getting a new phone or just want a backup on hand? Swinn's built-in CSV export lets you save a full copy of your data anytime, in a format any spreadsheet app can open, and bring it back in later with a single import. It's a good habit either way. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data does clear what's stored locally, but with export always a tap away, that's a choice that's entirely yours to make and undo, never something that catches you by surprise.

3. Permissions

Swinn may ask permission to send you local notifications on your device, for example a reminder that a recurring payment is due, or a heads-up that you're approaching your monthly budget. These are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. Granting this permission doesn't send anything anywhere; it only lets your device show you a notification it already created locally.

4. Third Parties

No analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no trackers of any kind. Swinn simply doesn't talk to anyone but you.

5. Children's Privacy

Swinn is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, adult or child, since it does not collect data from any user.

6. Future Changes

If a future version of Swinn introduces a feature that needs data to leave your device, for example an optional cloud backup, this policy will be updated in full detail before that feature ever launches, and you'll be asked for your explicit, separate consent the moment you choose to use it. Nothing about how your data is handled changes without that update coming first.

7. Contact
